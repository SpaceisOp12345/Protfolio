My Process

How commissions work
Step #1

Accept Commission Request

You reach out via Discord or Roblox with your idea. I review it and if it's something I can do, I accept the commission and we move forward.

Step #2

UI Explanation & Planning

I explain my UI approach — layout, color scheme, style direction, and any references. We align on the vision before any work begins.

Step #3

10% Upfront Payment

Before I start working, a 10% upfront payment is required. This secures your spot and shows commitment from both sides.

Step #4

Deliver Finals

I complete the work and send you the final files — screenshots, videos, or the actual UI. Revisions can be discussed if needed.

Step #5

Final Payment

Once you're happy with the delivery, the remaining balance is paid. After that, the commission is officially complete!
