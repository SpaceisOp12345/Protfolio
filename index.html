My Process

How commissions work
Step #1

Accept Commission Request

You reach out via Discord or Roblox with your idea. I review it and if it's something I can do, I accept the commission and we move forward.

Step #2

UI Explanation & Planning

I explain my UI approach — layout, color scheme, style direction, and any references. We align on the vision before any work begins.

Step #3

UI Preview in Roblox

The UI will be put into a Roblox game for you to see and interact with. This way you can experience the design in-game before anything is finalized.

Step #4

10% Upfront Payment

Before sending the files, a 10% upfront payment is required. This payment confirms the commencement of work and activates the delivery timeline.

Step #5

Deliver Finals

I send the completed work. Revisions can be discussed if needed.

Step #6

Final Payment

Once you're happy with the delivery, the remaining balance is paid. After that, the commission is officially complete!
